How it works

Reads every review like a script, line by line.

Your text is cleaned and converted into TF-IDF features, then classified by a Logistic Regression model trained on 50,000 IMDB reviews. If the model isn't confidently Positive or Negative — usually a sign of mixed praise and criticism — the review is reported as Neutral instead.
